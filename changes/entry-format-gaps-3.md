A form can take its field or its value from an argument, chosen from a closed list the form declares, so the pruning shrub prunes with one form and the sand garden brushes with one. A form can need that any of several fields differs from a value: a smooth sand garden refuses to be smoothed again. Plants are drawn on plant parts (stem, leaf, bloom, fruit, root, shoots) and raked sand on furrows, not on a creature's limbs.
