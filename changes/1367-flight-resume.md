A Soul left standing partway up a stair can walk again: its next walk carries on up or down the stair from where it stands, instead of failing every time. When the world frees a Soul stuck on a stair, it now sets it down on the nearer landing's floor, never in the air beside the stair. It moves at most 4 m, and its owner is told how far; with no clear landing that close it stays on the stair, is told why, and its next walk carries on from there.
