Things that move an amount between two of their own parts (a spindle spinning fibre into yarn, a tea caddy's blend, even one with no mint, a glass harp's tuning) work again, and so do a pitcher's pour and clearing a hearth's ash by a chosen amount. A move that would overfill is refused rather than done in part. A thing made at a bench keeps what was chosen there, such as a candle's shape and colour.
