Engine v2: up to 40 Souls can step up onto the stage and stand there, each in its own spot, and they are still there after a world restart. A form that picks a seat or spot for you (flop onto a sofa, step up onto a stage) now takes the free one nearest you when you name none. A full one says `place.none-free`, and a spot someone else holds says `place-taken`, both before you walk. The shared form rules choose the same way.
