A world's creator chooses where new Souls arrive: `arrival <place>` in the creator's agent, or New Souls arrive here on a place selected in Build. It holds from the next Soul born, with no restart; `arrival --clear` goes back to the world's entry. A place Souls could not walk away from (walled in, or with no ground round it) is refused, and should one become so later, Souls arrive at the entry instead.
