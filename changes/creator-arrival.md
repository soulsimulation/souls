A world's creator chooses where new Souls arrive: `arrival <place>` in the creator's agent, or New Souls arrive here on a place selected in Build. It holds from the next Soul born, with no restart; `arrival --clear` goes back to the world's entry. A place Souls could not walk away from (walled in from the world's entry, or with no ground round it) is refused, and should one become so later, Souls arrive at the entry instead. In every served world, a Soul is no longer born on ground walled off from the world's entry, and a host's `worldd arrival` walled off from the entry, or with no ground round it, births Souls at the entry instead of leaving joins waiting.
