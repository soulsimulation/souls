Things react, for Soul Actions: a kind may carry rules, written as data, that answer what happens to a thing or to a thing linked to it (a wall switch working its lamps, street lamps at dusk, a kettle on a lit stove, a shop opening when its timer comes due, a door chiming), from one closed pair of lists shared with Engine v2, with a check that refuses rules that could feed themselves or that act on a body, an offer or a draw.
