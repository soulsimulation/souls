Eden's churches, chapel, roundhouses, lighthouse, windmills, wind turbines, stairs, bridges and water are written as entries: a Soul can go into a church, climb to the lighthouse's lantern, go up a flight of stairs, cross a bridge, walk round a pond or along a canal, and stop or start a windmill. Eden's flower pots and potted plants become one carried kind that grows like the planter: water it and a day later it flowers.
