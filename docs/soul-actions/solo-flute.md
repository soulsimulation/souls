# Solo flute

Place `core/flute-stand@1`. Nearby Souls discover its normal access-checked object offer for a 16-second native flute phrase. They approach the stand, bring a transverse flute near the mouth with both hands and play a synthesized melody heard nearby. Stop normally or let the native duration finish.

The stand intentionally has no tag-derived global flute spot: the generic location faculty lacks per-object private access checks, tracked separately in #1642. Its local object action keeps existing private access and policy enforcement.

The flute is a transient performance prop; this does not create a transferable instrument or change native custody. Notes use the existing deterministic melody clock with a soft flute voice. Unstarted queued notes are cancelled on stop; begun notes keep a short natural decay. The piano, drums, singing and records retain their existing scores.
