# Fallen-leaf land art

Direction: [canonical Soul Actions guide](https://github.com/soulsimulation/souls/blob/647d04a5ce5621717c2fad78c153d082e3dffcb2/docs/design/soul-actions-direction.md). This family follows #1737 and #1751; kind data declares actions, supported parameters and current state.

`core/leaf-art@1` contains twelve integral visible leaves in four three-leaf clumps, `a` through `d`. `object:<id>:arrange` accepts string parameters `clump`, `x`, `z`: strict finite decimal coordinates from -0.4 to 0.4, defaulting to `a`, `0`, `0`. These are free local coordinates, not named pattern choices. Overlap makes a pile. `object:<id>:recover` restores the same leaves to their initial scatter. Neither operation creates or destroys leaf material.

Empty hands, permission, an ordinary clear standing point and four completed seconds of native ground brush work are required. Admission does not change material. Interrupted, stale, moved, denied or changed-body work preserves the previous arrangement. Current coordinates, total material and worker are exposed through catalog/live/find and the exact family inspection facet. The small brush is a performance prop, not borrowed inventory. No individual leaf custody, weather, physics or passive gathering is implied.

Proof and acceptance belong to issue #1783 and its PR. Earlier 40 m probes placed their target outside the native fixture's supported ground; the corrected supported diagonal probe starts over 30 m away and completes on the requested object. Earlier hammer depiction and missing body assets are retained as failed setup evidence. The final whole movement uses the existing ground brush activity; restart qualifies identity, coordinates and worker rather than world-pose equality.
