# Finite-water glass harp

`core/glass-harp@1` is a fixed tabletop instrument with three integral struck glasses and one six-unit reservoir. Tuning redistributes the same water; lowering a glass returns its water to the reservoir. The glasses and reservoir remain visible when empty.

The #1737/#1751 catalogue and ordinary `find` publish `object:<id>:tune` and `object:<id>:strike`. Both accept string `glass` (`1`, `2`, `3`, default `1`); tuning also accepts string `level` (`0`–`3`, default `1`). Current state gives all levels, remaining stock, transfer and strike records, worker and requirements. Cold exact IDs use ordinary walking from supported ground or a useful family refusal. Access, empty hands, the same body/start state and supported native reach remain authoritative.

Only three completed seconds of tuning transfer water. Only a completed one-second strike records and sounds the selected glass at its current authored pitch. Interruption, stale state, invalid choices, insufficient water and refused commits preserve material and records. Unrelated God Undo/redo preserves completed Soul work through the reviewed completion adapter.

The existing procedural game bell and live master bus sound the authored discrete note; water lowers its pitch. This is bounded musical interaction, without continuous fluid simulation or calibrated acoustic claims. The integral glasses and water are not independent held objects. Functional audio proof uses actual game-bus recording, normal playback and focused pitch/emitter controls; subjective listening and tonal quality remain unverified.
