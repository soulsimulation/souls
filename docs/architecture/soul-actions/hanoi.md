# Portable Tower of Hanoi

[Canonical direction](https://github.com/soulsimulation/souls/blob/647d04a5ce5621717c2fad78c153d082e3dffcb2/docs/design/soul-actions-direction.md), #1737 and #1751 apply. This is one puzzle capability with three integral rings on a separately portable board, rather than loose-ring custody or a new support/physics system.

`core/hanoi-board@1` declares `object:<id>:move-ring` with supported string `from` and `to` parameters: `a`, `b`, `c`, left to right; defaults are `a` and `c`. Only the top ring moves; larger-on-smaller, empty source and same peg refuse usefully. `object:<id>:reset` returns the same material to a. Seven legal moves can solve the three-ring puzzle on c. Every completed move or reset increments the lifetime operation counter. Solve count and last solver remain durable across later work/reset; current worker is separate.

Place the SAME board on an ordinary compatible table, with free working hands and normal access. `core/hanoi-table@1` is an ordinary fixed surface sized for it; other compatible surfaces are allowed. Actual native board support and pose determine work, including after pickup and placement. Two seconds of native operation must finish before the atomic ring/render-parameter/history update. Admission, interruption, changed owner/body/inputs/support, blocked standing room or rejected commit preserve the previous arrangement. No exact ring grasp or continuous loose-ring movement is claimed.

Production catalog/live and unmodified client publish actual IDs, parameters, defaults and current arrangement. The bounded current inspection facet describes pegs, rules, moves, solver and requirements. Same-object pickup/placement retains puzzle state. Same-data restart proof is scoped to current identity/facts, with native placement separately tested; no world-pose equality inference.

Proof, retained failures and acceptance belong to #1790 and its PR. The initial generic table slot correctly refused the board size; the authored compatible surface preserves old revisions and thresholds. An initial capture selector omitted the parent's name; it was corrected to the actual published full name. Original offscreen final handling is retained alongside the final ordinary follow recording. The requested hand-weight proposal was parked at intake because existing cosmetic lift poses do not drive real held-item hand points; no engine or custody bypass was added.
