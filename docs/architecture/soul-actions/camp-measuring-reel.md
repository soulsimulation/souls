# Camp measuring reel

`core-camp-measuring-reel@1` contains 80 integral decimetres of cord. Put it on a real accessible tabletop and find two real fixed `core-survey-stake@1` objects. `object:<reel-id>:measure` takes required string `from` and `to` exact stake IDs. Its ordinary native route visits the reel, both stake fronts and returns for four seconds of work. Only a completed valid route records their authoritative horizontal centre span (0.5–8 m) and allocates cord rounded **up** to whole decimetres. `object:<reel-id>:rewind` recovers the whole measured loop through four seconds of completed handwork. Another span requires rewinding first.

Cord remains integral on the same portable tool: stock coil on the left, loose measured loop on the right. No loose output is born or attached to a foreign object. The representation promises no taut world-spanning line, endpoint attachment, load-bearing, line collision or optical/precision measurement. This sizes finite material to real campsite layout, beyond manually chosen cord lengths or stored compass/map coordinates.

`coilDm + lineDm = 80`. Live scalar state exposes both material quantities, completed counters, latest metres/allocated units and endpoint snapshot coordinates.
